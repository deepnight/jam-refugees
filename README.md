# Refugees (Desert Bus jam)

In Refugees, you play a small family trapped inside a city. Every night, bombers from the goodies alliance will throw their peace bombs over your home. You have to survive these, gather food and find a way to escape this hell.

Note: the young boy walks more slowly and needs more food than its parents.

This game was created in 48h for the charitable jam Desert Bus.

If you like this game, please donate to the Secours Populaire.

Music credit: Epiq (Multifaros, https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Multifaros/Epiq_EP)

Website: https://deepnight.net/games/refugees/
